# Philippines (PHL) — eTravel

## Portal
- **Name:** Philippines eTravel
- **URL:** https://etravel.gov.ph
- **Account required:** Yes (email verification)
- **Fee:** Free
- **Output:** QR code sent via email

## Form Structure
The Philippines eTravel has 5 sections:

### 1. Personal Information
- Last name, first name, middle name (as on passport)
- Date of birth, sex (Male/Female)
- Nationality, country of residence
- Occupation (dropdown includes OFW — Overseas Filipino Worker — a Philippines-specific category; also Balikbayan status for returning Filipinos)

### 2. Passport Information
- Passport number, issue date, expiry date
- Country of issue

### 3. Travel Information
- Purpose of travel (Tourism, Business, Balikbayan, Visiting Relatives, Transit, Education, Employment, Medical, Official, Other)
- Date of arrival, flight number / vessel name
- Port of arrival (7 major airports: MNL Manila, CEB Cebu, CRK Clark, DVO Davao, ILO Iloilo, KLO Kalibo/Boracay, PPS Puerto Princesa/Palawan)
- Country of origin (last departure)
- Intended length of stay (1–30 days for visa-free)
- Address in Philippines, local contact number

### 4. Health Declaration
- Countries visited in last 30 days (note: 30 days, not 14)
- Currently experiencing fever, cough, cold, sore throat (yes/no)
- Close contact with sick persons in last 14 days (yes/no)

### 5. Contact Information
- Permanent home address, phone number, email

## Country-Specific Notes
- **Account required:** Unlike most countries, eTravel requires email registration and verification
- **Save progress:** eTravel allows saving progress and returning later (unusual for arrival cards)
- **Group registration:** One registration can include accompanying minors
- **Balikbayan:** Special purpose category for Filipinos returning from abroad — eligible for 1-year visa-free stay
- **OFW:** Overseas Filipino Worker — a distinct occupation category on the form
- **Health period:** 30 days lookback for countries visited (longer than the typical 14 days)
- **Visa-free:** Up to 30 days for eligible nationalities (extendable)
- **QR code:** Sent via email after submission; present at immigration
- **Passport validity:** Must be valid for at least 6 months from arrival
- **Kalibo (KLO):** Gateway airport for Boracay Island travelers
- **Puerto Princesa (PPS):** Gateway for Palawan / El Nido travelers
