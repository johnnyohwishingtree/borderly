# Indonesia (IDN) — Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD)

## Portal
- **Name:** Electronic Customs Declaration (e-CD)
- **URL:** https://ecd.beacukai.go.id
- **Account required:** No
- **Fee:** Free
- **Output:** QR code to present at customs

## Form Structure
The Indonesia e-CD has 6 sections:

### 1. Personal Information
- Family name, given name (as on passport)
- Date of birth, gender (Male/Female)
- Nationality
- Occupation (dropdown: Employee, Entrepreneur, Government Official, Professional, Student, Retired, Housewife/Homemaker, Diplomat, Other)

### 2. Passport Information
- Passport number, issue date, expiry date
- Passport issuing country

### 3. Flight Information
- Date of arrival, flight number
- Port of arrival (8 major international airports: CGK Jakarta, DPS Bali, SUB Surabaya, UPG Makassar, KNO Medan, JOG Yogyakarta, BPN Balikpapan, MES Padang)
- Country of departure

### 4. Accommodation
- Address in Indonesia
- City of destination (Jakarta, Bali, Surabaya, Yogyakarta, Bandung, Makassar, Medan, Semarang, Lombok, Other)
- Duration of stay (1–30 days for visa on arrival)

### 5. Customs Declaration
- Currency exceeding IDR 100,000,000 (~USD 6,300) — yes/no + amount
- Goods exceeding duty-free allowance (USD 500/person) — yes/no
- Animals, fish, plants or products thereof — yes/no
- Narcotics, psychotropic substances, firearms, explosives — yes/no
- Commercial goods — yes/no
- Alcohol quantity (duty-free: 1L per adult)
- Tobacco quantity (duty-free: 200 cigarettes or 25 cigars or 100g tobacco)

### 6. Contact Information
- Home address, phone number, email

## Country-Specific Notes
- **Family policy:** One declaration can cover a family traveling together
- **Strict drug laws:** Indonesia has very severe penalties for narcotics (including death penalty) — this makes the narcotics declaration field particularly important
- **Visa on arrival:** 30 days, extendable once for another 30 days (IDR 500,000)
- **Currency threshold:** IDR 100,000,000 (approximately USD 6,300)
- **Duty-free allowances:** USD 500 per person, 1L alcohol, 200 cigarettes / 25 cigars / 100g tobacco
- **QR code output:** After submission, travelers receive a QR code to present at customs
- **Bali travelers:** Most use Ngurah Rai International Airport (DPS)
- Passport must be valid for at least 6 months from arrival date
