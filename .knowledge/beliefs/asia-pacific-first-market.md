# Belief: Asia-Pacific is the right first market

## Status
Working assumption

## Statement
Prioritizing Asia-Pacific countries (Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Korea, Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia, India, Australia, New Zealand) as the initial schema set is the right go-to-market strategy.

## Evidence
- 11 of 14 supported countries are Asia-Pacific
- Founder's travel patterns and domain knowledge are strongest in this region
- Several APAC countries have digitized portals (Visit Japan Web, SG Arrival Card) that are well-suited to auto-fill
- APAC travel volume is growing faster than other regions

## What would confirm
- Early user base skewing APAC-traveler
- Higher engagement/completion rates for APAC schemas vs Western ones
- APAC portals being more stable and auto-fill-friendly than expected

## What would invalidate
- Early users primarily traveling to USA/UK/Canada/EU
- APAC portals being too unstable or changing too frequently
- Western country schemas being higher demand from actual users

## Referenced by
- `src/schemas/` — 11 APAC schemas vs 3 Western (USA, GBR, CAN)
- `.knowledge/domain/countries/` — 15 country knowledge files
- `.knowledge/patterns/add-country.md` — country addition workflow
